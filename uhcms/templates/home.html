About Campus Helpdesk

Campus Helpdesk is a simple web platform where students report university problems and helpdesk staff resolve them. Every complaint gets a unique ID, a clear status and a comment thread, so nothing is lost and everyone knows what is happening.

Frequently asked questions

Who can submit a complaint?

Any registered student. Create an account with your university email.

Who can see my complaint?

You and the helpdesk staff. Other students cannot see it.

How do I know it is being fixed?

Open the complaint to see its status and staff comments.
